# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project should be able to discuss and contribute without harassment or discrimination. This policy applies to issues, reviews, discussions and other spaces maintained for the project, and to people representing it.

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, offer useful feedback, accept corrections and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Disagreement about a technical decision is welcome; insults, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment and repeated unwanted contact are not. Do not publish anyone's personal information without permission.

## Reporting and moderation

Maintainers are responsible for moderation. When safe, flag a problematic public comment or open a brief issue requesting moderation without repeating private information. No dedicated private incident-reporting address is configured. For sensitive incidents, first ask a maintainer to arrange a private channel without posting identifying details. GitHub's own abuse-reporting tools remain available for conduct on its platform.

Maintainers should consider reports fairly, protect reporters' information and explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Responses may include asking for a correction, removing content, limiting participation or banning a participant, based on severity and repeated behavior. A reporter or affected participant can request reconsideration with relevant context. This project does not promise confidential handling through a public issue or a fixed response time.

## Source

This project-specific policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html). Its reporting and moderation text is tailored to the channels actually available to this project.
